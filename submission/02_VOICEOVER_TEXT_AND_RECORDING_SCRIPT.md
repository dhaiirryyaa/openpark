## 💡 Quick Tips for Recording Your Voice
1. **Mic Position:** Keep the microphone 6 to 8 inches from your mouth at a slight 45° angle to prevent "P" and "B" plosives.
2. **Energy & Cadence:** Smile slightly while reading Segment 01 and Act 5; it brings natural warmth and conviction to your tone.
3. **Pauses:** Let the natural comma and period pauses breathe for half a second. The automated video timeline is built with 0.15s – 0.25s silence pads between segments to match your breath.
4. **Custom Audio Upload:** When you record your own audio file, save it as `submission/assets/founder_voice.wav` or `submission/assets/founder_voice.m4a`, and we can instantly sync it to the exact frame timeline!
