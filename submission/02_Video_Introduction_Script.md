## 🎥 Recording Guidelines & Production Tips for the Founders

1. **Camera Framing & Eyeline:**
   - Shoot in landscape mode (16:9) at eye level.
   - Keep eyes in the upper third of the frame.
   - Look directly into the camera lens, not at the screen.

2. **Audio Setup (Crucial):**
   - Use a lapel / collar microphone or a dedicated USB condenser mic.
   - Record in a quiet room with minimal echo (soft furnishings, curtains help).

3. **Lighting:**
   - Position a key light or soft natural light in front of you (at roughly 45 degrees).
   - Avoid sitting directly in front of bright windows (prevents silhouetting).

4. **B-Roll & Screen Recording Ingestion:**
   - Record a smooth 30-second screen capture of the live app at:
     `https://openpark-web-production.up.railway.app/app/`
   - Show:
     1. Search bar autocomplete ("MG Road" or "Koramangala")
     2. Map pin fly-to & place preview card
     3. 1 km AI Scan button discovering vacant parking spots
     4. Navigation route line drawing smoothly to destination.

5. **Export Settings:**
   - Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p Full HD)
   - Frame rate: 30 fps or 60 fps
   - Codec: H.264 MP4 with AAC audio
   - Target file size: Under 300 MB (fits comfortably in Google Drive)
   - Recommended Filename: `02_OpenPark_Founder_Video_Introduction.mp4`
