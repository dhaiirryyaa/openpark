Proposed Solution
03 / 10

OpenPark: The Zero-Hardware Smart Parking Grid

Unlocking visible commercial lots and invisible micro-spaces into one synchronized mobility engine.

🔍 1 km AI Space Scanner

Proprietary spatial algorithm continuously indexes a strict 1,000-meter radius around any destination to discover official parking lots, vacant private grounds (45–550 m²), and verified curbside bays.

⏱️ 15-Minute Slot Lock

Drivers reserve their parking spot ahead of arrival with guaranteed availability. Real-time delta updates prevent double-booking and eradicate the anxiety of arriving at a full facility.

🧭 OSRM Road Router & GPS

Turn-by-turn road guidance navigating drivers directly to the parking gate—not just the building facade. Powered by live Traccar vehicle telemetry and an offline-first service worker cache.

No \$15,000 hardware per lot required • Driver PWA + Lightweight Operator Tablet Dashboard OpenPark 2026
